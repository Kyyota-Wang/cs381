Yunlong Wang · arXiv preprint, September 2026

An intervention-conditioned world model that calls a digital health campaign's year-end prescription volume from its first four weeks — and, when the future media plan is unknown, imagines its own and forecasts just as accurately. With an account of where that capability stops.
